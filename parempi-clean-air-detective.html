The challenge

The project’s research on how traffic emissions affect health is technical. The brochure had to explain it to children without losing the facts, and to leave them with things they can do.

What I did

  • A journey in five stepsThe inside spread follows a path from where pollution comes from to what everyone can do about it, with one illustration for each step.
  • A character to followA young detective with a magnifying glass guides the reader, and appears again on the cover.
  • The project’s identityThe brochure follows the green identity of PAREMPI and includes the QR codes and the European Union funding notice.
