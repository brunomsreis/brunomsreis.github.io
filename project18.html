📱🎨 FocusFlow

Focus Flow is a personal time management app designed as a kind of agency for organizing and sharing tasks.

Type
Personal project, mobile app
Role
Branding, UX and UI design
Tools
Figma
Platform
Designed primarily for mobile

Why I built it

It was born out of my own need for an alternative to traditional tools like Google Calendar, offering more interaction and engagement through gamification and integration with other users.

What it does

  • Lets people share tasks with other users.
  • Shows a graphical representation of the progress and status of completed tasks.
  • Uses gamification and interaction with other users to keep people engaged.

The aim is to enhance productivity by combining efficient task management with social and interactive elements.

How I made it

I started by researching similar applications on the market, then designed the branding, UX and UI myself in Figma. The project is entirely personal.
