Privacy & Cookies

Who is responsible

This is the personal portfolio website of Bruno Reis. For any question about your data, write to brunomiguelsantosreis@gmail.com.

What this site collects

The site has no forms, accounts or comments, so it does not ask you for personal data. The only thing it can measure is how it is used, and only if you agree.

  • Analytics (only if you accept): Google Analytics 4 records page views, clicks on projects and time spent on a page. It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_* and uses anonymised IP addresses. Nothing is loaded from Google Analytics until you click "Accept".
  • Your choice: it is stored in your browser (localStorage, key cookie-consent), not in a cookie. The light/dark mode preference is stored the same way. Neither is sent to me.

Third-party content loaded by the site

Some content is served by other companies, so your browser contacts their servers (and your IP address reaches them) when a page loads, independently of the analytics choice:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) for the typography.
  • Archive.org for the videos embedded in some project pages.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site and keeps standard server logs.

Links to LinkedIn, Behance, GitHub and Instagram only leave this site when you click them.

Your choices and rights

  • You can accept or decline analytics at any time: . Declining removes the Google Analytics cookies from this site.
  • Under the GDPR you can ask for access, correction or deletion of your data, and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD.
