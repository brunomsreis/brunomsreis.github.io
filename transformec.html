Green finance carousel

A carousel that explains green finance to industrial SMEs, made for TransformEC.

Client
TransformEC
My role
Infographic and carousel design
Deliverables
A 15-slide carousel for social media, in three chapters
Year
2026

The challenge

Green finance is full of acronyms, programmes and criteria, and SMEs in industry rarely have time to read about it. The carousel had to turn it into something clear enough to scan on a phone, and useful enough to act on. It is written in Portuguese.

What I did

  • Three chaptersThe content is split into Understand, Choose and Apply, with a navigation pill on each cover so the reader always knows where they are.
  • Dense information made lightIcons, short cards and comparison tables replace paragraphs, and dark and light slides alternate to set the rhythm.
  • One systemA single grid, icon set and teal accent, with the project logos always in the same place at the bottom.

The slides

Tap a slide to enlarge it.

1. Understand (Perceber)

What green finance is, how to judge environmental sustainability and which investments count as green.

2. Choose (Escolher)

Which European and national programmes exist, how they differ and which one fits a project.

3. Apply (Candidatar)

Five steps to prepare an application, and what to do and what to avoid.
