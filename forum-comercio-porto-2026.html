The challenge

The forum was built around participation: visitors answered questions such as “How do you imagine the commerce of the future?” by writing their ideas on post-its and sticking them on a wall. The event needed to be documented as it happened, and its look carried into the posts, outdoor panels and web pages around it.

What I did

  • Photographed the eventI covered the day: the installations, the ideas written by the participants and the atmosphere of the venue.
  • Made videosVideos of the event, published on YouTube.
  • Designed for social mediaPosts to announce and report on the forum, in the event’s blue and yellow identity.
  • Designed for outdoor and webPanels for MUPIs and banners for the event website, adapted to each format.
