🎨 WAIT Magazine — Editorial & Photography

WAIT is an independent photography magazine created from scratch, blending travel, storytelling, and visual culture. Each issue is dedicated to a journey — from the Camino de Santiago to the landscapes of Sardinia — and explores the intersections between people, places, and memory. This collection showcases editorial spreads, triptychs, interviews, and collaborative features, with a focus on photography as both documentation and creative expression. Designed as both print and digital, WAIT is a space where images breathe, stories unfold, and journeys continue beyond the page.
